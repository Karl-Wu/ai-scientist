---
name: scientific-result-note
description: Draft a note on what a scientific result supports, the uncertainty it leaves, and the next check.
---

Use the supplied result and the user's focus to write an evidence-bounded research note.

1. Identify the comparison, outcome, or hypothesis addressed by the result when the source provides it.
2. Preserve relevant estimates, units, sample sizes, uncertainty intervals, and reported test statistics. Explain what those observations support in the context of the analysis.
3. Separate the reported observation from the author's interpretation. Call out missing design information, uncertain measurement, or limits on interpretation only where they matter to the requested focus.
4. Suggest a concrete follow-up check when the available evidence leaves a substantive question. Label it as proposed work.

Use a short narrative or the sections requested by the user. Keep unreported values and methods absent from the findings.
